Construction Highlights

Built with stronger foundations, smarter systems, and premium finishing standards.

01

Structure, Foundation & Roof

The structural package is planned around stable foundations, anti-termite protection, RCC performance, and a carefully layered steel-and-tile roofing system.

Soil Treatment & Foundation

  • Expansive black cotton soil removed up to hard strata, approximately 6 feet, for structural stability.
  • Retaining wall or CRS wall provided to plinth level based on soil conditions.
  • Strip footings executed on hard strata with RCC columns raised up to plinth level.
  • Controlled morrum backfilling in compacted layers followed by RCC plinth beam and grade slab.

Standards referenced: IS 1498, IS 1904, IS 456:2000, IS 370 Part 2

Anti-Termite Treatment

  • Pre-construction soil treatment with approved chemicals before the grade slab stage.
  • Treatment planned as per IS 6313 Part 2 for protection against subterranean termites.
  • Post-construction drilling at basement junctions with chemical emulsion injection at regular intervals.

Superstructure, Steel & Roofing

  • RC framed structure designed in line with seismic compliance requirements for strength and durability.
  • Anchored MS columns with J-bolts, aligned ridge members, rafters, tie beams, and purlins.
  • 12mm V-board sheets at ridge and tie beam joints with aluminium purlins.
  • Waterproof polypropylene sheets with sealed overlaps, Monier interlocking cement roof tiles, ridge tiles, mortar capping, and waterproofing at joints.

Standards referenced: IS 456:2000, IS 1893 Part 1:2016, IS 13920:2016, IS 2062, IS 800:2007, IS 13827, IS 3067

Quality Control

  • Third-party or in-house cube testing for concrete.
  • Welding inspection for fabricated joints.
  • Curing quality and alignment verification before finishing.

02

Walls, Plaster & Exterior Finish

Masonry, plastering, reinforcement bands, and waterproof exterior treatments are planned to support durability, thermal comfort, and crack resistance.

Exterior Walls

  • Red brick masonry built over a concrete DPC bed for damp protection.
  • 9-inch thick external walls planned for strength and thermal insulation.
  • Concrete stiffener bands at every 1.0m height for uniform load transfer and improved crack resistance.
  • Double coat cement plaster at approximately 18 to 20mm total thickness.

Standards referenced: IS 1077, IS 2212, IS 2185 Part 1 & 2, IS 456:2000, IS 1542

Interior Walls

  • 4-inch thick internal brick partition walls.
  • Single coat cement plaster around 12 to 15mm thick.
  • Final putty or skim coat preparation for primer and emulsion finish.

Boundary Wall

  • RCC columns with masonry infill on a concrete DPC bed.
  • Concrete stiffener bands at 1.0m intervals for added stability.
  • 15 to 18mm cement mortar plaster on both sides with exterior waterproof paint.
  • Expansion joints to absorb thermal movement and reduce surface cracking.

Quality Control

  • Plaster thickness measured against specification.
  • Surface evenness checked during finishing.
  • Paint adhesion and moisture resistance verified.

03

Flooring & Tiling

Flooring is organized by area use, balancing premium visual finish with wet-area safety, drainage, and long-term durability.

Living / Dining / Lounge
Premium vitrified, large format 800×800mm or 600×600mm, polished finish aligned with project theme.
Bedrooms
Matching premium vitrified flooring, with optional tile or wood-finish skirting depending on final finish.
Kitchens & Utility
Vitrified tile with anti-skid ceramic or vitrified options and dado up to 600mm above the platform.
Bathrooms & Wet Areas
Vitrified or anti-skid ceramic flooring with wall tile dado up to approximately 1.2m to 1.5m.
Veranda
Anti-skid tiles or full-body vitrified finish with proper slope and drainage protection.

Quality Control

  • Tile alignment and slopes checked with spirit level.
  • Joint spacers used for consistency.
  • Adhesive setting and curing verified before handover.

Standards referenced: IS 13755, IS 15622, IS 13829

04

Doors, Windows & Glazing

Door and window specifications focus on durability, smooth operation, premium appearance, and better light and sound performance where needed.

Main Entrance & Internal Doors

  • Main door with solid hardwood or engineered wood frame and veneer finish.
  • Quality hardware package including locks, hinges, handles, and deadbolt.
  • Internal flush doors with veneer or laminate finish.

Standards referenced: IS 2202 Part 1 & 2, IS 6245

Windows & Glass

  • uPVC window frames in sliding or casement configuration as per design.
  • Mosquito mesh provision included.
  • Tempered glass for large windows and double-glazed units for larger openings or sound insulation zones.

Standards referenced: IS 2835, IS 3548

Quality Control

  • Door alignment and shutter movement checked.
  • Hardware tightening verified.
  • Glass inspected for cracks and sealed at edges.

05

Kitchen & Countertops

The kitchen package is planned around durable countertops, practical utility points, and a cleaner daily-use workflow.

Counter, Sink & Fittings

  • Granite or quartz countertops, approximately 20 to 30mm thick, with polished edges.
  • Stainless steel under-mount sink in single or double bowl options depending on layout.
  • Provision for chimney or exhaust, dishwasher or washing machine, and water purifier.

Standards referenced: IS 1062, IS 303, IS 1237

Quality Control

  • Counter slope checked.
  • Cabinet alignment verified.
  • Sink leakages tested and hardware load checked.

06

Sanitary, Plumbing & Bathrooms

Bathroom and plumbing specifications combine premium sanitary selections with concealed service routing and durable drainage systems.

Sanitary Ware & CP Fittings

  • Wall-hung, counter, or under-counter wash basins depending on location.
  • Western WC with dual flush and premium brand selections such as Jaquar or CERA.
  • Premium chrome-plated, brushed, or matte black mixers, showers, and taps.

Standards referenced: IS 1742, IS 10144

Plumbing & Drainage

  • Concealed CPVC or PVC plumbing as per certified material standards.
  • Sewer drainage in PVC or HDPE.
  • Rainwater lines planned in line with regulatory requirements.

Standards referenced: IS 4985, IS 1785

07

Electrical, Lighting & Power

Electrical planning focuses on concealed wiring, reliable switchgear, adequate appliance points, and LED-based lighting for everyday use.

Wiring & Switchgear

  • Concealed copper wiring with IS or BIS certified materials.
  • Load calculations planned villa-wise.
  • Modular switches from premium brands such as Legrand, Schneider, or Havells.

Standards referenced: IS 694, IS 1293, IS 513

Points & Lighting

  • Adequate power points for ACs, refrigerators, microwaves, and kitchen appliances.
  • LED fixtures planned for living areas, outdoors, corridors, and common areas.

Standards referenced: IS 302 Part 1

Quality Control

  • Insulation resistance test carried out.
  • Earthing verification completed.
  • MCB tripping checks and circuit load tests performed.

08

Backup, Water & Utility Services

Utilities are designed to support comfortable long stays with backup power, treated water, pressure systems, sewage treatment, and rainwater management.

Power & Water Systems

  • 100% DG backup planned.
  • Overhead tanks for individual villas and underground sumps for the community.
  • Treated or filtered RO drinking water provision.
  • Hydro-pneumatic system for more uniform water pressure.

Standards referenced: IS 3370 Part 2, IS 10500, IS 3025

Sewage & Rainwater

  • Sewage Treatment Plant with treated water reused for flushing and landscaping.
  • Rooftop rainwater harvesting and recharge pits distributed across the project.

Standards referenced: IS 4011

Quality Control

  • Generator load tests performed.
  • Water quality analysis checked.
  • STP treated water quality verified.

09

Amenities & Common Areas

Shared spaces are designed around landscaped leisure, active recreation, security, and community use, while still supporting everyday comfort.

Landscape & Clubhouse

  • Landscaped gardens with native species, walking paths, and seating areas.
  • Clubhouse with multipurpose hall, indoor games, badminton court, skating ring, and gym with branded equipment.

Pool & Security Infrastructure

  • Main swimming pool plus kids’ pool with deck, hygiene, and filtration systems.
  • Gated entrance, CCTV coverage, boom barriers, guard house, and solar fencing.

Standards referenced: IS 15622, IS 13087, IS 3043

Quality Control

  • Pool water pH testing and hygiene checks.
  • Gym equipment safety inspection.
  • CCTV coverage audit across critical areas.

10

Finishes, Painting & Aesthetics

The finishing palette is planned for consistent surface quality, long-term weathering performance, and a cleaner premium appearance inside and out.

Interior Walls

  • Two coats of putty over plastered walls for a smooth and leveled base.
  • One coat of interior wall primer.
  • Two coats of tractor emulsion paint.
  • Brand reference: Asian Paints Tractor Series.

Exterior Walls

  • Texture base coat for bonding and weather resistance.
  • One coat of exterior-grade primer.
  • Two coats of Apex Suprema or Apex Ultima for waterproof and UV-resistant performance.
  • Brand reference: Asian Paints Apex Series.

Metal Work

  • Two coats of enamel paint on metal elements.

Quality Control

  • Paint shade consistency checked.
  • Adhesion tests performed.
  • Weatherproof coating inspection completed.
